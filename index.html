Introduction

Welcome to my co-op reflection for Fall 2024! I was employed at the University of Guelph during this term as a Junior Analyst in DevOps Change Management. Utilizing Microsoft Azure DevOps to enhance work management and streamline the software development lifecycle was the main objective of my position.

I gained hands-on experience with Agile processes, .NET/VBA development, and Power Automate/RPA. This opportunity enhanced my technical and communication skills while exposing me to real-world software development practices.

Employer

Supporting more than 30,000 students, teachers, and staff, the Computing and Communications Services (CCS) department at the University of Guelph is the main supplier of IT services. CCS is renowned for its dedication to innovation and digital transformation, making sure the university's IT infrastructure can adapt to changing administrative and academic demands.

I finished my co-op semester with the CCS Enterprise Applications team, which is responsible for creating and managing the fundamental systems for student, research, financial, and human resources services. This entails creating automated processes, facilitating cloud-based development, and guaranteeing the dependability of enterprise-level software. To improve collaborative development, automate deployments, and streamline task management, the team makes use of solutions like Microsoft Azure DevOps.

Along with providing technical expertise, the team helps the university implement agile methods to enhance project completion. CCS assists the institution in providing scalable, secure, and user-friendly solutions that support both staff and student everyday operations by incorporating contemporary development approaches.

By contributing to.NET/VBA projects, I hoped to increase my coding abilities and my capacity to actively participate in Agile stand-up meetings, including sprint reviews and daily stand-ups. By providing team members with regular check-ins and succinct project updates, I also hoped to improve my communication abilities.

I also set out to investigate IT Change Management and Robotic Process Automation (RPA) utilizing Power Automate in order to broaden my technical expertise. I selected these technologies because they are crucial to contemporary DevOps workflows and provide chances to improve productivity by streamlining tedious activities.

Job Description

My primary responsibility as a Junior Analyst in DevOps Change Management at the Computing and Communications Services (CCS) of the University of Guelph was to assist the team in streamlining software development workflows and task management procedures. Utilizing Microsoft Azure DevOps to help with task tracking, repository management, and facilitating team collaboration through Agile principles constituted quite a bit of my job.

Contributing to the creation and enhancement of apps with.NET and VBA was one of the most fascinating parts of my job. To provide effective solutions for automating procedures and optimizing data processing, these initiatives required coding expertise and analytical thought. This was especially fulfilling to me since it made it possible for me to connect my theoretical understanding with real-world applications.

I took part in Agile stand-ups and sprint reviews in addition to technical work, which gave me important insights into project management and teamwork. In order to increase team productivity, I also had the chance to test out Robotic Process Automation (RPA) with Power Automate, automating repetitive operations and daily reporting.
