Introduction

Welcome to my co-op reflection for Summer 2025! I was employed at the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement(MPBSD) during this term as a Junior Analyst within the Labour and Transportation I&IT Cluster, Digital Solutions Branch. My role focused on supporting software development, accessibility testing, and IT coordination in a multi-platform environment.

Through this position, I gained practical experience in applying my technical knowledge to real business solutions while contributing to the delivery of government digital services.

Employer

The Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery (MPBSD) supports Ontarians by providing a wide range of digital and in-person services, including business registration, land and property information, consumer protection, and public safety.

I worked within the Labour and Transportation I&IT Cluster, Digital Solutions Branch, which focuses on developing and maintaining technology solutions for government programs and services. This branch plays a critical role in ensuring reliable, accessible, and secure applications for both internal staff and the public.

The team operates in a collaborative environment that blends traditional mainframe technologies with modern tools such as SharePoint and Azure DevOps. This combination provided me with exposure to both legacy systems and contemporary platforms, reflecting the unique challenges and opportunities of working in a large-scale government IT environment.

Goals

My goals for this work term were designed to connect directly with my role as a Junior Technical Analyst and to help me grow as both a technical contributor and a collaborator. I developed goals that aligned with my job tasks in quality assurance, accessibility testing, automation, and documentation. Specifically, I wanted to:

  • Gain proficiency in AODA accessibility testing by running multiple test cycles with tools such as WAVE and NVDA.
  • Strengthen my knowledge of automated testing frameworks by learning how to create and edit test scripts.
  • Improve my ability to document and report defects by contributing to test cases and bug tracking through Azure DevOps.
  • Develop stronger team collaboration skills by actively engaging in Agile meetings, including daily stand-ups and sprint planning.
  • Enhance my technical communication by writing internal documentation and knowledge base articles that could support teammates and onboarding.

These goals were chosen because I wanted to build skills that will directly benefit my next work experience. Accessibility testing sharpened my ability to design inclusive solutions, while automation skills prepared me for more advanced testing projects. Learning Azure DevOps gave me practical experience with a widely used industry tool, which will be valuable in both academic and professional settings. Participating in Agile workflows improved my collaboration and adaptability, both of which are essential in modern software development.

Reflecting on these outcomes, I successfully completed accessibility test cycles, contributed test cases and bug reports in Azure DevOps, and participated consistently in Agile meetings. I also wrote internal documentation that was reviewed and shared with the team. The one area where I did not reach the full depth I had hoped for was in automated testing frameworks. While I gained exposure to editing and running scripts, I was only able to build a limited number of new scripts from scratch. Overall, I achieved most of my goals and laid a strong foundation to expand on in future work terms.

Job Description

As a Junior Technical Analyst with the Labour and Transportation I&IT Cluster, Digital Solutions Branch, my role involved supporting both technical and coordination activities across multiple projects. I contributed to accessibility testing by running AODA/WCAG compliance checks with tools like WAVE and NVDA, ensuring applications met accessibility standards. I also gained experience with automated testing frameworks, where I edited and built test scripts under supervision to strengthen the team’s quality assurance processes. A major part of my work included using Azure DevOps to write and track test cases and defect reports, which taught me how to clearly communicate issues and follow them through the resolution process.

Beyond testing, I participated in Agile meetings such as daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives, where I shared progress and blockers with the team. This allowed me to practice collaboration and improve my comfort working in a structured development environment. I also contributed to technical documentation, updating onboarding and knowledge base materials to support the team’s workflow.

The most interesting aspects of this role were the combination of working with both legacy technologies (such as mainframe-based systems) and modern tools (such as SharePoint and Azure DevOps), and seeing how both are essential to government IT. The job required skills in analysis, problem-solving, technical writing, and communication. Some of these I had been introduced to in class, but I learned how to apply them in a real-world, professional setting during the work term.

Conclusion

This work term gave me valuable exposure to the responsibilities of a Junior Technical Analyst and helped me grow in both technical and professional areas. I achieved most of the goals I set at the start of the term, including improving my skills in accessibility testing, defect management, Agile teamwork, and documentation. While I made progress with automated testing frameworks, I see this as an area I want to continue building on in future terms.

If someone were to describe my experience, I would want them to say that I learned how to apply classroom knowledge in a government IT setting, contributed meaningfully to quality assurance and project coordination tasks, and developed skills that will serve me in future roles. Overall, this position strengthened my confidence in both technical problem-solving and team collaboration, and it reinforced my interest in pursuing a career at the intersection of software development, quality assurance, and IT project support.
