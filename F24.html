Introduction

Welcome to my Fall 2024 co-op reflection! During this term, I worked as a Junior Analyst in DevOps Change Management at the University of Guelph. My role focused on leveraging Microsoft Azure DevOps to improve work management and optimize the software development lifecycle.

I gained hands-on experience with Agile processes, .NET/VBA development, and Power Automate/RPA. This opportunity enhanced my technical and communication skills while exposing me to real-world software development practices.

Employer

The University of Guelph’s Computing and Communications Services (CCS) department is the primary provider of IT services, supporting over 30,000 students, faculty, and staff. CCS is known for its commitment to digital transformation and innovation, ensuring the university’s technological infrastructure meets evolving academic and administrative needs.

The CCS Enterprise Applications team, where I completed my co-op term, focuses on developing and maintaining core systems related to HR, Financial, Student, and Research services. This includes designing automated tasks, supporting cloud-based development, and ensuring the reliability of enterprise-level applications. The team uses tools such as Microsoft Azure DevOps to streamline work management, automate deployments, and enhance collaborative development.

In addition to technical expertise, the team supports the university’s adoption of agile practices to improve project delivery. By integrating modern development methodologies, CCS helps the university deliver scalable, secure, and user-friendly solutions that support the daily operations of students and staff alike.

Job Description

As a Junior Analyst in DevOps Change Management at the University of Guelph’s Computing and Communications Services (CCS), my role centered around supporting the team in optimizing work management processes and enhancing software development workflows. A significant portion of my work involved leveraging Microsoft Azure DevOps to assist with tracking tasks, managing repositories, and supporting team collaboration through Agile practices.

One of the most interesting aspects of my role was contributing to the development and improvement of applications using .NET and VBA. These projects required analytical thinking and coding skills to design efficient solutions for automating workflows and streamlining data processes. I found this particularly rewarding because it allowed me to bridge theoretical knowledge with practical implementation.

In addition to technical tasks, I participated in Agile stand-ups and sprint reviews, which provided valuable insights into project management and collaboration in a team environment. I also had the opportunity to experiment with Robotic Process Automation (RPA) using Power Automate, automating daily reports and repetitive tasks to improve team efficiency.
